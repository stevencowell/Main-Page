16 steps, in order
Prepare on the computer · STEP 01 OF 16

Choose the trial file

  1. Extract the whole guide folder from the ZIP before using its files. Open the folder named files and choose Woodlands-TRIAL-400mm.dxf.
  2. Keep the original 800 mm file for later. Do not shrink that file in SheetCam, and do not scale the trial's machine program up for the final cut.
  3. Plan a flat, identified 3 mm mild-steel offcut. A 400 x 400 mm square is only the finished design envelope; allow extra usable stock for the actual lead-ins, kerf and workholding.
What you should see

The selected drawing is the complete 400 mm trial, with all three name lines, foliage, two waves and two hanging holes.

Prepare on the computer · STEP 02 OF 16

Put the actual machine record beside you

  1. Use the course's completed setup record: exact SheetCam tool and post-processor, 3 mm material recipe, measured kerf and where compensation is applied, leads/path rules, and the TAP destination folder.
  2. Check that the record also covers the fitted torch and consumables, physical current, air, sensing, THC/Kerf states, safe movement, dry run, stopping, cooling and electrical isolation.
  3. If anything is missing, you can prepare and inspect the drawing on a computer disconnected from the cutter. Obtain the missing values or demonstration before connecting or operating the machine.
What you should see

A usable record for this machine and this material, rather than numbers copied from a screenshot.

Prepare on the computer · STEP 03 OF 16

Import into a fresh SheetCam job

  1. Open SheetCam. Save any existing work, then choose File > New job.
  2. Choose File > Import drawing. Select Woodlands-TRIAL-400mm.dxf and click Open.
  3. In Drawing options, choose Metric and the bottom-left dot in the Drawing position grid, then OK. Keep the drawing at its supplied size; do not introduce a custom scale.
What you should see

The complete sign appears in SheetCam. Its job origin is now the bottom-left of its 400 x 400 mm bounding box, not the circle centre.

Prepare on the computer · STEP 04 OF 16

Check size and position before adding toolpaths

  1. Choose Zoom to fit current part. Select Scroll screen, right-click the drawing and choose Measure.
  2. Check the circle left-to-right and top-to-bottom: both should be 400 mm. Use opposite extreme points, not a diagonal. Press Escape after measuring.
  3. Check the two hanging holes are 12 mm diameter. If checking centre-to-edge, that is a 6 mm radius. After bottom-left placement, the hole centres are at (90,345) and (310,345) mm.
  4. Compare the artwork with the supplied preview. There must be no dimension lines, a second sign, open fragments or missing letters. If measurements are unclear, verify in CAD before proceeding.
What you should see

A 400 mm circle, two 12 mm holes and the intended artwork. Screen appearance alone is not a size check.

Prepare on the computer · STEP 05 OF 16

Create the cutting operation

  1. Under Operations, click the jet-cutting icon to open Jet cutting operation.
  2. Use the course's Outside Offset whole-part method and select layer CUT, which contains the supplied closed contours. Select the exact tool from the completed record for this material and thickness.
  3. Check Feed rate, Path rules and lead-in/out type and length against that record. Arrange starts in waste material; a lead must fit its opening without crossing retained lettering or a bridge.
  4. Click OK. Never copy the example tool, feed, material thickness or lead length shown in the supplier picture.
What you should see

The operation uses CUT and the correct recorded tool. Every internal opening has a waste-side path; the perimeter path lies outside the sign.

Prepare on the computer · STEP 06 OF 16

Watch the whole preview, then make the TAP

  1. Use Show input paths, Show tool paths, Show path directions, Show cut start points and Show rapid moves. Inspect the small letters and bridges closely.
  2. Run simulation in SheetCam. This is a screen preview. Check the trial's 38 closed contours: 35 artwork openings and two mounting holes first, then the outside circle last. Each contour must cut once. Check all paths and leads fit the usable offcut and miss clamps.
  3. If a cut is missing, doubled, open or on the wrong side, correct the drawing/operation and replay the whole preview. Do not add a second operation blindly.
  4. Choose File > Save job as and save a clearly named trial job. Verify the supplier-confirmed post, then File > Run post processor. Save Woodlands_TRIAL_400_v1.tap. Resolve any processing error before continuing.
  5. Copy that exact TAP revision to the machine-computer folder in the record. Keep the DXF, editable SheetCam job and TAP together. Renaming a DXF to .tap does not convert it.
What you should see

The exact checked trial TAP is in the correct folder. The job was previewed all the way through, with internal cuts first and the circle last.

At the machine · STEP 07 OF 16

Load the offcut using the recorded procedure

  1. Complete the machine's pre-use checks: clear dry area, flammables removed, serviceable guarding, screens, extraction, access controls and emergency arrangements.
  2. Keep the required electrical isolation in place for loading. Use the recorded lifting/handling protection. Support and secure the flat 3 mm offcut so the cut and waste pieces will not cause a collision.
  3. Match the actual material, torch, consumables, current, air and work-return connection to the setup record. Keep clamps and the complete cut envelope clear of travel.
  4. Follow the recorded power-on sequence. Everyone remains outside the controlled travel area; start extraction at the specified point before any firing.
What you should see

Identified, secure stock with enough usable area for the complete trial toolpath and leads, and a physical setup matching the job record.

At the machine · STEP 08 OF 16

Connect and reference SureControl

  1. Open SureControl. In the documented fresh-start sequence it initially reads Disconnected. Click Connect; the expected state is Offline and Connect changes to Reset. Do not press Reset just because the label changed.
  2. With the prepared system clear of people, enable Online. The documented fresh start then expects Not Referenced. Resolve any unexplained state or fault before continuing.
  3. Click Reference. Use the axes and sequence confirmed for this installation. Click Start only with the travel area clear, wait for normal completion, then OK.
  4. Referencing establishes machine positions; it does not position the sign on the sheet.
What you should see

The required axes are referenced, with no unexpected motion or unresolved fault. Offline is not electrical isolation.

At the machine · STEP 09 OF 16

Load the exact trial TAP

  1. Click Load. In Load Program, use Directories to find the confirmed folder and select Woodlands_TRIAL_400_v1.tap.
  2. Leave Import DXF unticked: the cutting paths have already been prepared in SheetCam. Click OK.
  3. Check both the filename and complete sign preview against your trial job. Confirm the expected 400 mm job and orientation. Do not press Start yet.
What you should see

The correct trial program is loaded. A fresh unpositioned job may show Set Origin, as described in the supplier reference.

At the machine · STEP 10 OF 16

Check sensing and the recorded torch test

  1. Perform the Touch sensor test recorded for the fitted sensing system. Touch in Indicators shows the response; Touch in Options enables the function. They are different controls.
  2. For the manual's contact-test method only where confirmed for this machine: use the recorded low jog speed and clearance, Page Down gently to sensing contact, release immediately, then Page Up to the recorded clearance. Check the indication changes and clears. Do not hold Shift or force the torch into the sheet.
  3. Follow the record on whether a torch test is required. If required, use its recorded position, clearance, duration, screens and protection. Click Torch On/Off once to begin and click the same icon again to stop at the recorded point; verify the arc is out. A blank test entry is not a reason to experiment.
What you should see

Sensing responds normally, the torch is safely clear, and the specified test is complete or recorded as unnecessary. Resolve a fault before going further.

At the machine · STEP 11 OF 16

Set the lower-left job origin

  1. Identify the lower-left corner of the 400 x 400 mm design envelope used in SheetCam. Choose a position that leaves space for all kerf, lead-ins and travel as well as the sign.
  2. At the recorded safe clearance and low jog speed, use the recorded X/Y jogging controls to reach that position. For the documented keyboard method, arrows move X/Y while held; release to stop. Do not hold Shift.
  3. Release the keys, then click Set Origin. Do not click Origin, which commands movement to a previously assigned zero.
  4. Check the part coordinates and orange crosshairs match the intended job zero. The next step verifies the real travel.
What you should see

The origin is assigned to the planned lower-left design-envelope corner. The 400 mm circle fits the offcut with space for the complete toolpath.

At the machine · STEP 12 OF 16

Run the complete dry run

  1. Use the dry-run conditions and clearance in the setup record. Confirm the travel area is clear, Online is enabled and Simul is ON/highlighted.
  2. Click Start from the beginning of the program. If the start-menu wording is unclear, cancel and have the trained operator identify it; do not choose a restart or nearest-profile option for this fresh job.
  3. Watch the whole sequence from the screened operator position. The machine moves, but the torch must not fire. Check every internal path, the outside circle, leads and travel stay clear of edges and clamps.
  4. Wait for completion and Idle. Leave Simul ON while checking. If the program or origin changes, repeat the complete dry run.
What you should see

The full trial route completes without firing, obstruction or unexpected travel. SureControl Simul moves the actual machine; it is different from SheetCam's screen preview.

At the machine · STEP 13 OF 16

Make the trial cut

  1. Recheck the filename, origin, actual 3 mm sheet, torch/consumables and physical current against the record. Any change since the dry run requires the checks to be repeated.
  2. Open Cut Parameters using the sliders icon and compare the relevant values with the completed record. Confirm which values the TAP supplies and which SureControl applies. Do not overwrite CAM-controlled settings or apply kerf compensation twice.
  3. Read the Options states against the record. The documented plasma setup has Touch ON and Laser OFF; THC and Kerf must have the individually recorded states. Confirm Online ON. Deliberately turn Simul OFF only when ready for the real cut.
  4. With extraction running and access secured, remain at the screened controls and start from the beginning. Observe the whole cut. For faults, lost extraction or abnormal behaviour, use the recorded stop/emergency procedure; do not improvise a restart.
What you should see

All internal openings are cut before the perimeter. The trial cut finishes under the recorded machine conditions.

Inspect and decide · STEP 14 OF 16

Finish, isolate and remove safely

  1. Wait for motion to stop, the arc to be out and the controller to report Idle. Keep access controlled; Idle is not electrical isolation.
  2. Follow the recorded shutdown, cooling/post-flow and extraction run-on sequence. Apply and verify electrical isolation using the established method before access or retrieval.
  3. Use the recorded cooling and handling method, tools and protection. Treat the sign, waste and remaining sheet as hot and sharp. Do not touch the metal to test its temperature or reach into an energised table.
What you should see

Safe isolated equipment and a trial sign retrieved under the recorded handling conditions.

Inspect and decide · STEP 15 OF 16

Decide whether you are happy with the trial

  1. After safe cleaning, check every opening has cut through. All letter centres, bridges and gum-leaf details should remain attached; no melted-through connections or partly detached details.
  2. Measure the circle in two directions against 400 mm and the hanging holes against 12 mm. Record actual values and compare with the tolerance agreed for your machine and hardware.
  3. Look for unacceptable bowing, excessive dross, bevel, gouged starts or rough lettering. Stand back and judge whether the words, foliage and overall appearance work for you.
  4. Record the tool/recipe, TAP revision, measurements and a photo. If anything is unsatisfactory, stop the full-size job. Have the cause corrected, regenerate a new trial TAP and repeat the preview and dry run before retesting.
What you should see

A deliberate decision: HAPPY TO PROCEED or REVISE AND RETEST. Finishing paint will not repair a weak bridge or incomplete cut.

Inspect and decide · STEP 16 OF 16

Prepare the original 800 mm sign as a new job

  1. Only if you are happy with the trial, start a fresh SheetCam job and import Woodlands-FULL-800mm.dxf. Use Metric and the same bottom-left drawing-position method.
  2. Confirm 800 mm diameter in both directions, two 12 mm holes and 39 closed contours in total: 36 artwork openings, two mounting holes and the outside circle. Do not enlarge the trial DXF or TAP; use this original full-size drawing.
  3. Use the checked material/tool setup, then rebuild and review leads, path rules, heat distribution and cut order for this larger job. Save a separate job and Woodlands_FULL_800_v1.tap.
  4. Repeat the complete machine setup, file/origin checks and full dry run on the actual larger sheet. The trial supports your decision but does not prove the 800 mm job's placement, heat behaviour or toolpaths.
  5. Cut, isolate, retrieve and inspect using the same checked sequence. Keep both trial and full-size records.
What you should see

A separately checked full-size job, cut only after your trial decision and the full-sheet checks are satisfactory.
