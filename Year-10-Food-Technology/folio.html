Evidence card 01 · Food Equity

Equity evidence brief

Compare access for two fictional households using one credible source, a small evidence table or an annotated map. Identify one limitation in your evidence and a fair next question.

Evidence to keep: Evidence table or annotated access map with a short caption explaining what it can and cannot show.

Your decision: Which barrier is best supported by your evidence, and what else would you need to know?

Evidence card 03 · Food for Special Occasions

Occasion concept

Create a small occasion menu concept after checking the people, meaning, access needs and limits of the brief. Ask those involved rather than assigning meaning to a whole culture.

Evidence to keep: A labelled menu concept or decision table with a caption naming the intended people and purpose.

Your decision: Which menu decision best responds to the people and occasion?

Catering service review
